# Undead Holy Oof

### Casting a Holy spell hurts undead casters in the WoW lore, so why not be fully immersed!

This addon plays a pain sound every time you cast a Holy spell as an Undead Paladin or Priest.

---

**Install:** Extract the addon into `Interface/AddOns/UndeadHolyOof/`, restart WoW, and enable it in the AddOns list.

- Click the minimap skull or type `/uhoof` to open settings.
- Choose **0% (mute), 25%, 50%, 75%, or 100%** volume.
- `/uhoof test` previews a sound; `/uhoof on` and `/uhoof off` toggle automatic playback.

No sound? Check the addon volume and WoW’s Master volume.
